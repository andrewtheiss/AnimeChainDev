# Get Started

Create your AnimeChain account in under a minute. Sign in with your email — using Google or a one-time email link — then choose how you want to use AnimeChain.

<div data-widget="onboarding"></div>

## How it works

1. **Sign in with your email.** Google sign-in is instant; the email option sends you a one-time sign-in link (no password to remember). Authentication is handled by Firebase Auth.
2. **Pick your path:**
    - **I have a crypto wallet** — link your existing wallet to your email with [Sign-In with Ethereum](sign-in-with-ethereum.md). You sign one gasless message; the signed record stays in your browser.
    - **Just use my email** — no wallet, no extensions, nothing to install. Your account is ready immediately.

## Which should I choose?

| | Wallet (Sign-In with Ethereum) | Email only |
|---|---|---|
| Requirements | MetaMask or another browser wallet | Just your email |
| Setup time | ~1 minute | Seconds |
| Best for | Crypto-native users who already hold ANIME | Everyone else |

You can always start with email and link a wallet later by coming back to this page.
